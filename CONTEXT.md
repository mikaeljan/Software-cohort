# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy courses and work through their lessons.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund request**:
A learner's ask to have the money they paid for one purchase returned. It is always Pending review, Refunded, or Refund declined. A purchase has at most one refund request.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund**:
The return to a learner of the full amount they paid for a purchase.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending review**:
A refund request that has not been decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending chargeback, Requested, Open

**Refunded**:
A refund request that was approved, with the money returned to the learner.
_Avoid_: Approved, Reversed, Cancelled

**Refund declined**:
A refund request that was turned down.
_Avoid_: Rejected, Denied
