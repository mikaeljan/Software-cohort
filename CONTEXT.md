# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy courses and work through their lessons.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund request**:
A learner's ask to get back the money they paid for one purchase of one course, which a person reviews by hand and either approves or declines. It is always in exactly one of three states: Pending review, Refunded, or Refund declined.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund**:
The money sent back to the learner's card once their refund request is approved.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending review**:
The state of a refund request that has been submitted but not yet approved or declined; no money has moved.
_Avoid_: Pending chargeback, Requested, Open

**Refunded**:
The state of a refund request that was approved and whose refund has been sent.
_Avoid_: Approved, Reversed, Cancelled

**Refund declined**:
The state of a refund request that was turned down; no money moves.
_Avoid_: Rejected, Denied
